# Invoice system

Open **Workspace → Invoices** (`/invoices`). Admins and operators create and manage billing; viewers can read and print. BD managers can view and download all invoices, create drafts, and edit only drafts they created. Issuing, voiding, deleting, payments and shared billing settings remain restricted to admins and operators. Each invoice has an authenticated detail URL at `/invoices/<id>`.

Create a draft with seller and customer billing details, tax IDs, currency, dates, PO reference, line items, terms, and bank instructions. Search CRM customers by company name or email, then choose their billing contact. Save reusable customer billing details and payment terms, and shared seller defaults. Save the draft, review, and issue it. Issuing locks the document and does not send an email. Use **Print / Save PDF** to download a copy through the browser print dialog.

In the email inbox, the invoice icon sits beside the catalog icon in the expanded reply toolbar. It opens an invoice dialog for the selected correspondent and conversation while preserving the email draft. Compose also offers **Create invoice** beside **Add catalog PDF** for a single entered recipient. Exact contact email matches resolve the CRM company; company names are never used to guess an identity. Invoice creation, including AI reply invoice PDFs, is available to admins, operators and BD managers.

Company profiles show invoice history and balances, with a **Create customer invoice** shortcut. Linked invoices point back to the company and source email conversation. Issuing, recording payments, and voiding add events to the company timeline. Billing activity does not automatically change sales pipeline stages.

The inbox invoice button opens recent existing invoices for the correspondent, with invoices linked to the current conversation first. Matches use the conversation ID, resolved CRM company, and exact billing email for older unlinked invoices; customer names are not used to guess ownership. **Edit draft** opens the existing document inside the dialog, reads its current revision/status, and saves to the same invoice. Issued and void invoices cannot be edited. **Create new invoice** remains separate. Closing or editing invoices preserves the email reply draft. The list shows up to 50 recent results, with a full customer invoice history link when a company is known.

**Attach PDF** generates the latest saved invoice on the server and adds it to the current reply or compose draft. Newly saved invoices also offer **Attach PDF to email**. PDFs include locally embedded fonts, the actual brand wordmark, bank snapshot, QR code, balances, and creator signature. Draft and void files are clearly named and marked. Reattaching the same invoice replaces its older attachment. Existing file limits (10 attachments, 7.5 MB per file) apply; switching conversations during generation cancels attachment to the old reply. The user sends the email separately.

The product picker uses active database products, variants, currencies, MOQ, and `price-tier` metadata. Quantity pricing updates when quantities cross saved tier thresholds. An individually priced variant keeps its own quote when it differs from the parent price. Manual agreed prices are marked explicitly, and the server checks linked product/contact identities, currency, and current automatic prices before saving. There is no automatic currency conversion. Saved customer catalogs can add their currently available products; their historical PDF prices are not treated as current quotes.

Record payments after money arrives. Partial payments reduce the balance; paid and overdue states are derived from the balance and due date. Payment methods describe external receipts; this feature does not charge cards or move money. Unpaid issued invoices can be voided. Only drafts can be deleted. Payment reversals, credit notes, recurring billing, and automated email delivery are not implemented.

Fully paid issued invoices automatically display a red circular **PAID / Thank you** stamp with a scalloped edge and worn ink texture over the amount due on screen, in print, and in generated email PDFs. The stamp is a translucent overlay and adds no document space. The same transparent artwork is used in the browser and PDF. The stamp uses the existing derived paid status; partial, unpaid, draft, and void invoices do not display it.

## Amounts and rounding

Choose **Receiving bank account** in the invoice form to use the bank details supplied for Xinrui Manufacturing Technology Limited: HSBC account ending 3055, CCB Asia USD ending 7118, or CCB Asia HKD ending 7100. CCB currency choices must match the invoice. The HSBC account currency was not specified in the supplied letter. You can still select manual payment instructions. Bank details are stored in the server-only `invoice_bank_accounts` table and returned only through authenticated billing context. On draft creation or save, a database trigger resolves the selected account and copies its details into the invoice document; submitted replacement details are ignored. Issuing or recording payments preserves that snapshot even if the saved bank account changes later.

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261023021720_invoice_bank_accounts.sql` for the three accounts and snapshot trigger (already applied to the linked project). Verify with `tests/invoice-banks.test.ts` and the rollback-only `tests/invoice-banks-database.sql`.

Printed payment details show the beneficiary, account number, bank name, SWIFT/BIC, and account currency or bank code where provided. Bank and beneficiary addresses remain available in the editor and saved invoice data, but are omitted from the invoice paper to keep it concise.

The invoice uses a black-and-white layout with the Filazoo wordmark, striped line items, and payment details. Positive outstanding balances appear in red; paid and void balances remain neutral. The original creator's workspace display name is captured in `invoices.creator_name` on creation and rendered automatically in a locally hosted handwriting font above the bold title “Authorised Sales Manager.” Draft edits, payment updates, and later profile renames preserve that signature name. Existing invoices were backfilled from their recorded creator's current workspace name. If no name is available, the signature area stays blank instead of substituting another user. This is a visual signature, not a cryptographic signing workflow.

The QR is generated locally as vector artwork and opens the existing authenticated invoice route; it is not a payment QR or a public customer portal. The QR uses `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`; local development URLs fall back to `https://crm.filazoosupply.com` so printed codes do not point at a phone's localhost. The invoice routes must be deployed there before scanning production links will work. Scanning still requires a CRM account with billing access. Payment instructions and notes come from the saved document.

USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, CAD, AUD, and HKD are supported. Money is stored in integer cents and quantities are whole units. Each item has a tax percentage (basis points internally). The invoice discount is allocated proportionally across items using cumulative integer allocation, then tax is rounded to the nearest cent per line. Shipping is untaxed. Supported limits: 100 items, 1,000,000 units per item, 1,000,000 currency units per unit price/discount/shipping/payment, and 1,000,000,000 currency units subtotal.

Customer and seller details are snapshots, so later changes elsewhere in the CRM cannot change issued documents. Numbering uses a database identity sequence (`INV-000001` format). Gaps from deleted drafts or rolled back inserts are expected. Dates use UTC calendar dates consistently on the server.

## Backend

- `GET /api/invoices?page=0&search=customer&status=draft&companyId=<uuid>`: 25 records per page, newest first, with optional customer scope. Status filters accept `draft`, `issued` (including paid/partial/overdue), or `void`. Dashboard balances cover all matching records and group currencies separately, independently of pagination.
- `GET /api/invoices/context`: validated, authenticated customer/product search, billing defaults, customer catalogs, and conversation/customer prefill. Search results use 25-record pages; explicit product SKU selections support up to 100 products.
- `PUT /api/invoices/billing`: save customer billing defaults or shared seller settings; requires a billing write role.
- `POST /api/invoices`: validates a complete document and creates a draft; the server calculates its total.
- `GET /api/invoices/<uuid>`: document and payment history.
- `GET /api/invoices/<uuid>/pdf`: authenticated PDF generated from the latest saved document; private/no-store response with filename and revision headers. Uses local Inter, Allison, and Noto Sans SC font assets, included in deployment tracing.
- `PATCH /api/invoices/<uuid>`: `save`, `issue`, `void`, `delete`, or `payment`. Draft changes and transitions require the current `revision`. Payments require a UUID idempotency key.

Tables: `invoices`, `invoice_payments`, `invoice_events`, `customer_billing_profiles`, `invoice_settings`. All use RLS with no browser grants, matching the existing server-only CRM architecture. API routes validate active membership and billing roles. `mutate_invoice` is executable only by `service_role`, runs as invoker, and checks the actor's current membership. It locks the invoice row before changing state or balances, rejects stale edits, duplicate-key conflicts, future payment dates, and overpayments. Status changes, edits, and payments write audit events in the same transaction. A database trigger maintains company/contact/thread references from the document and rejects cross-company contact links. Customer/contact deletion clears the live foreign key while preserving the original billing document.

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261022093507_invoice_system.sql` and then `supabase/migrations/20261023005013_connected_invoice_system.sql` to an existing database. Both are already applied to the linked project; their filenames match the remote migration history. Existing invoice documents are linked during migration only when their billing email exactly matches a unique CRM contact. Fresh installations can use `supabase/schema.sql`. No additional environment variables are needed beyond the existing Supabase configuration.
