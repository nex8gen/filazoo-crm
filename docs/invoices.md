# Invoice system

## Payment methods

Open **Settings → Payment methods** to add, edit, or remove up to ten bank transfer, PayPal, or other payment methods. Admins and operators manage them; other workspace members can view them. Each method has a display name, optional currency restriction, customer-facing details, and an optional HTTPS payment link. Settings use the existing server-only `invoice_settings` JSON storage; no migration is required. Seller-default and payment-method saves merge with version checks to preserve each other's fields.

Saved methods appear as compact cards. **Edit** opens a method's fields, and a successful save collapses them again. Bank transfer has separate beneficiary, account number, SWIFT / BIC, and bank name fields. PayPal displays its icon in Settings, the invoice selector, the customer invoice, and the PDF.

In the invoice editor, select one or more **Customer payment methods**. Only selected methods appear on the invoice, customer portal, and PDF, with clickable payment links. Details are stored as invoice snapshots; later edits or removals in Settings do not rewrite saved invoices. Remove and select a method again to use its latest details on a draft. Existing receiving bank accounts and manual instructions remain available. Currency-restricted methods must match the invoice currency. Payment links direct customers to the supplied provider; receipt is still confirmed and recorded by staff.

## Customer orders and payment before shipment

PO items can be selected from the active Products catalog by name or SKU, with variant/color, product identity, image, MOQ and quantity-tier pricing preserved. A manually entered unit price is marked as an agreed price. Custom items remain available. New/changed product lines are checked against the current catalog on the server; invoicing uses the saved agreed order price even for partial quantities or later catalog changes.

New POs fetch the latest saved company profile and show company name, linked contact, email, phone, billing address, tax ID and shipping details. **Refresh from saved profile** explicitly refreshes an editable order; save profile edits first. The saved order is a snapshot, so later company changes do not rewrite it. Its invoices use that snapshot's billing and product details. Older orders retain their saved shipping information; capturing newly introduced customer details requires saving the order.

Company profiles show invoices, payments, and billing activity to BD managers as well as other workspace members. The existing action permissions still apply: managers can create and edit their own invoice drafts; admins and operators issue/void invoices and record external payments.

Confirm a customer PO, then use **Create invoice for remaining items**. The saved draft links to the order and its individual lines, carrying the customer, agreed unit prices, currency, PO reference, and shipping snapshot. The invoice links back to the company's Purchase orders tab. Order shipping details appear on printed and PDF invoices.

Drafts reserve quantities, issued invoices count toward billing, and deleted drafts or void invoices release their quantities. Reduce quantities or remove lines in a draft for partial billing, then create another invoice for the remainder. The database locks the order while checking allocations to prevent concurrent duplicate billing. Linked order items, currency, customer, and PO reference cannot change while active invoices exist.

The default is **100% payment before shipment**. New billing defaults use zero credit days; existing saved customer terms remain intact. To use different order terms, select **Different agreed terms**, enter the required pre-shipment percentage, and describe the agreement. Before an order moves to Shipped or Completed, the database checks that all quantities are on issued invoices and their recorded payments cover the required percentage of the issued total. An explicitly documented 0% arrangement allows shipment before billing/payment. Fulfillment, invoice status, and payment balances remain separate. No historical invoices are linked merely by matching a text PO reference.

Open **Workspace → Invoices** (`/invoices`). Admins and operators create and manage billing; viewers can read and print. BD managers can view and download all invoices, create drafts, and edit only drafts they created. Issuing, voiding, deleting, payments and shared billing settings remain restricted to admins and operators. Each invoice has an authenticated detail URL at `/invoices/<id>`.

Create a draft with seller and customer billing details, tax IDs, currency, dates, PO reference, line items, terms, and bank instructions. Search CRM customers by company name or email, then choose their billing contact. Save reusable customer billing details and payment terms, and shared seller defaults. Save the draft, review, and issue it. Issuing locks the document and does not send an email. Use **Print / Save PDF** to download a copy through the browser print dialog.

In the email inbox, the invoice icon sits beside the catalog icon in the expanded reply toolbar. It opens an invoice dialog for the selected correspondent and conversation while preserving the email draft. Compose also offers **Create invoice** beside **Add catalog PDF** for a single entered recipient. Exact contact email matches resolve the CRM company; company names are never used to guess an identity. Invoice creation, including AI reply invoice PDFs, is available to admins, operators and BD managers.

Company profiles show invoice history and balances, with a **Create customer invoice** shortcut. Linked invoices point back to the company and source email conversation. Issuing, recording payments, and voiding add events to the company timeline. Billing activity does not automatically change sales pipeline stages.

The inbox invoice button opens recent existing invoices for the correspondent, with invoices linked to the current conversation first. Matches use the conversation ID, resolved CRM company, and exact billing email for older unlinked invoices; customer names are not used to guess ownership. **Edit draft** opens the existing document inside the dialog, reads its current revision/status, and saves to the same invoice. Issued and void invoices cannot be edited. **Create new invoice** remains separate. Closing or editing invoices preserves the email reply draft. The list shows up to 50 recent results, with a full customer invoice history link when a company is known.

**Attach PDF** generates the latest saved invoice on the server and adds it to the current reply or compose draft. Newly saved invoices also offer **Attach PDF to email**. PDFs include locally embedded fonts, the actual brand wordmark, bank snapshot, QR code, balances, and creator signature. Draft and void files are clearly named and marked. Reattaching the same invoice replaces its older attachment. Existing file limits (10 attachments, 7.5 MB per file) apply; switching conversations during generation cancels attachment to the old reply. The user sends the email separately.

Every saved invoice with a customer billing email includes its customer portal QR code, including drafts and generated PDFs. Drafts label the QR as activating when issued; scanning it before issue reveals no invoice data. The same QR works after issue without reprinting, provided the billing email is unchanged. Newly created and updated invoice responses include the link immediately. Previously downloaded PDFs must be downloaded again to include the QR.

The product picker uses active database products, variants, currencies, MOQ, and `price-tier` metadata. Quantity pricing updates when quantities cross saved tier thresholds. An individually priced variant keeps its own quote when it differs from the parent price. Manual agreed prices are marked explicitly, and the server checks linked product/contact identities, currency, and current automatic prices before saving. There is no automatic currency conversion. Saved customer catalogs can add their currently available products; their historical PDF prices are not treated as current quotes.

Record payments after money arrives. Partial payments reduce the balance; paid and overdue states are derived from the balance and due date. Payment methods describe external receipts; this feature does not charge cards or move money. Unpaid issued invoices can be voided. Only drafts can be deleted. Payment reversals, credit notes, recurring billing, and automated email delivery are not implemented.

Fully paid issued invoices automatically display a red circular **PAID / Thank you** stamp with a scalloped edge and worn ink texture over the amount due on screen, in print, and in generated email PDFs. The stamp is a translucent overlay and adds no document space. The same transparent artwork is used in the browser and PDF. The stamp uses the existing derived paid status; partial, unpaid, draft, and void invoices do not display it.

## Amounts and rounding

Choose **Receiving bank account** in the invoice form to use the bank details supplied for Xinrui Manufacturing Technology Limited: HSBC account ending 3055, CCB Asia USD ending 7118, or CCB Asia HKD ending 7100. CCB currency choices must match the invoice. The HSBC account currency was not specified in the supplied letter. You can still select manual payment instructions. Bank details are stored in the server-only `invoice_bank_accounts` table and returned only through authenticated billing context. On draft creation or save, a database trigger resolves the selected account and copies its details into the invoice document; submitted replacement details are ignored. Issuing or recording payments preserves that snapshot even if the saved bank account changes later.

Printed payment details show the beneficiary, account number, bank name, SWIFT/BIC, and account currency or bank code where provided. Bank and beneficiary addresses remain available in the editor and saved invoice data, but are omitted from the invoice paper to keep it concise.

The invoice uses a black-and-white layout with the Filazoo wordmark, striped line items, and payment details. Positive outstanding balances appear in red; paid and void balances remain neutral. The original creator's workspace display name is captured in `invoices.creator_name` on creation and rendered automatically in a locally hosted handwriting font above the bold title “Authorised Sales Manager.” Draft edits, payment updates, and later profile renames preserve that signature name. Existing invoices were backfilled from their recorded creator's current workspace name. If no name is available, the signature area stays blank instead of substituting another user. This is a visual signature, not a cryptographic signing workflow.

The QR opens a private customer portal at `/customer/invoices/<id>`. Issued invoices with a billing email receive a signed link; drafts have no active customer QR. Screen, print, downloaded PDFs, and AI-generated PDF attachments use the same customer link. `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` controls its origin; localhost falls back to `https://crm.filazoosupply.com` for phone scans. The portal is deployed on that custom domain. Payment instructions and notes come from the saved document.

Previously printed QR codes pointed at `/invoices/<id>`. Anonymous scans are redirected through `/customer/legacy/<id>` to the signed customer interface. New invoices are not automatically admitted to this compatibility registry. Revocation or a changed billing email disables the old link; customer passwords still gate return visits. The customer routes bypass the staff cookie refresh and staff authentication gate entirely. The existing QR on INV-000008 was verified against the live custom domain, including the automatic email association and password setup popup.

The first scan automatically associates the invoice billing email with a separate customer account, opens that invoice without a password, and prompts the customer to set one. The QR is a bearer credential: anyone holding it can view that invoice until its customer account is protected. Password setup uses an atomic first-claim update and invalidates earlier QR sessions. Subsequent scans require that customer's password; a scan cannot reset or overwrite it. Customer sessions never create staff users or workspace memberships. Each session is restricted to the scanned invoice, even when other invoices share its email. QR secrets are exchanged from the URL fragment and removed from browser history; cookies are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production. Links change if the invoice billing email or signing secret changes. `CUSTOMER_PORTAL_SECRET` optionally provides a dedicated signing key; otherwise the existing server Supabase secret is used.

Staff portal management lives in **Customer profile → Customer portal**. The left column lists that customer's issued invoices; selecting an invoice shows its payment balance, customer account security, actual linked order where present, and **Conversation** / **Delivery tracking** tabs. Each invoice has a compact **Manage in customer profile** shortcut that opens the correct customer and invoice. Deep links use `?tab=customer-portal&invoiceId=<uuid>`. The company API and scoped conversation/shipping API both check the invoice's live `company_id`; shared billing emails do not grant cross-company access.

For an issued invoice with no CRM customer link, **Create customer profile** connects a uniquely matched customer or creates a profile from the saved customer name and billing email. Staff can instead choose **Connect to an existing customer**. The service-role-only `connect_portal_invoice_customer` RPC requires a current active admin, operator, or BD manager; it locks the invoice and serializes connections by billing email. Repeated requests reuse the existing connection; ambiguous matches require an explicit selection, and an already-connected invoice cannot be reassigned. Existing messages and tracking remain under the same invoice ID. The issued document, amounts, signature, and QR keys are preserved. Installing migration `20261026074231_customer_portal_profiles.sql` adds only this explicit workflow; it does not merge or backfill existing customer records. Verify with `tests/company-portal-routes.test.ts` and rollback-only `tests/customer-portal-profiles-database.sql`.

Customers can read payment status and amounts, download their invoice, read the linked order's progress, view staff-entered carrier/location/tracking details, and send messages. Shipping locations are manual team updates and carrier links, not live GPS or automatic carrier synchronization. Invoice and payment mutations, CRM records, internal email threads, other customers, and settings remain behind staff authorization. Conversations and order/payment details refresh every 15 seconds. Staff reply and update shipment details in **Customer profile ? Customer portal**, using the selected invoice?s Conversation and Delivery tracking tabs; viewers have read-only access. Customers needing password recovery currently contact their sales representative.

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261026064917_customer_portal.sql` (already applied to the linked project). Its tables use RLS with public/anon/authenticated grants revoked, matching the server-only CRM model. The service-role-only rate-limit function runs as invoker and limits scans, password attempts, and messages across app instances. Credentials use salted scrypt, session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, and responses use private/no-store caching. Test with `tests/customer-portal.test.ts`, rollback-only `tests/customer-portal-database.sql`, and `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/verify-customer-portal.mjs` against the running local app. The integration script creates isolated test invoices and removes them afterward; it sends no emails.

USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, CAD, AUD, and HKD are supported. Money is stored in integer cents and quantities are whole units. Each item has a tax percentage (basis points internally). The invoice discount is allocated proportionally across items using cumulative integer allocation, then tax is rounded to the nearest cent per line. Shipping is untaxed. Supported limits: 100 items, 1,000,000 units per item, 1,000,000 currency units per unit price/discount/shipping/payment, and 1,000,000,000 currency units subtotal.

Customer and seller details are snapshots, so later changes elsewhere in the CRM cannot change issued documents. Numbering uses a database identity sequence (`INV-000001` format). Gaps from deleted drafts or rolled back inserts are expected. Dates use UTC calendar dates consistently on the server.

## Backend

- `GET /api/invoices?page=0&search=customer&status=draft&companyId=<uuid>`: 25 records per page, newest first, with optional customer scope. Status filters accept `draft`, `issued` (including paid/partial/overdue), or `void`. Dashboard balances cover all matching records and group currencies separately, independently of pagination.
- `GET /api/invoices/context`: validated, authenticated customer/product search, billing defaults, customer catalogs, and conversation/customer prefill. Search results use 25-record pages; explicit product SKU selections support up to 100 products.
- `PUT /api/invoices/billing`: save customer billing defaults or shared seller settings; requires a billing write role.
- `POST /api/invoices`: validates a complete document and creates a draft; the server calculates its total.
- `GET /api/invoices/<uuid>`: document and payment history.
- `GET /api/invoices/<uuid>/pdf`: authenticated PDF generated from the latest saved document; private/no-store response with filename and revision headers. Uses local Inter, Allison, and Noto Sans SC font assets, included in deployment tracing.
- `PATCH /api/invoices/<uuid>`: `save`, `issue`, `void`, `delete`, or `payment`. Draft changes and transitions require the current `revision`. Payments require a UUID idempotency key.

Tables: `invoices`, `invoice_payments`, `invoice_events`, `customer_billing_profiles`, `invoice_settings`. All use RLS with no browser grants, matching the existing server-only CRM architecture. API routes validate active membership and billing roles. `mutate_invoice` is executable only by `service_role`, runs as invoker, and checks the actor's current membership. It locks the invoice row before changing state or balances, rejects stale edits, duplicate-key conflicts, future payment dates, and overpayments. Status changes, edits, and payments write audit events in the same transaction. A database trigger maintains company/contact/thread references from the document and rejects cross-company contact links. Customer/contact deletion clears the live foreign key while preserving the original billing document.

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261022093507_invoice_system.sql` and then `supabase/migrations/20261023005013_connected_invoice_system.sql` to an existing database. Both are already applied to the linked project; their filenames match the remote migration history. Existing invoice documents are linked during migration only when their billing email exactly matches a unique CRM contact. Fresh installations can use `supabase/schema.sql`. No additional environment variables are needed beyond the existing Supabase configuration.
